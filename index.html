Risk flags

How the risk score works
  • Start at 0.
  • +2 if the run touched customer data.
  • +3 if a customer-facing action (Follow-up Agent) has no human approval.
  • +1 if the outcome was failed or escalated.
  • Level: low 0–1, medium 2–3, high 4+.
